Phytochemicals

Phytochemicals are natural chemical compounds produced by plants that provide protection against pests, stress, and disease. While not classified as essential nutrients like vitamins and minerals, they act as powerful antioxidants in the human diet and help reduce the risk of chronic illnesses.

(Heavily from AI Overview by Google)

Major Types and Food Sources

  • Carotenoids Orange, red, and yellow pigments (such as beta-carotene in carrots and lycopene in tomatoes) that support eye health and immune function.
  • Flavonoids Polyphenols found in berries, dark chocolate, tea, and red wine that offer anti-inflammatory and cardiovascular benefits.
  • Glucosinolates Sulfur-containing compounds found in cruciferous vegetables like broccoli and kale that assist in cellular detoxification.
  • Phytoestrogens Plant-based compounds found in soybeans and flaxseeds that mimic or modulate estrogen pathways.

Health Benefits

  • Antioxidant Action Neutralize unstable free radicals that cause cellular and DNA damage.
  • Disease Prevention Associated with lower rates of heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and certain cancers.
  • Immune Support Modulate inflammatory responses and enhance overall physiological resilience.
